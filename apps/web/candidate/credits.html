Original Noita Together

Noita Together 2 reuses and adapts source and artwork from Noita Together, by Soler91 and its contributors. The original MIT copyright notice (2022 Soler91) is preserved in distributed notices and applicable mod folders. This project does not claim exclusive authorship of that work.

Native transport and dependencies

The original project's transport used pollws by probable-basilisk (fakepyry/pyry). The current beta uses NT2's local WebSocket bridge and native wrappers; pollws source and DLLs are not part of its approved payload.

The desktop companion uses Tauri, Rust libraries and Microsoft WebView2. The service uses Fastify, PostgreSQL libraries and other open-source packages. Release archives include project notices and the generated dependency licence inventory/text bundle for their exact shipped dependencies.
